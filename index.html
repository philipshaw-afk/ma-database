    How these figures are calculated
    Source
    M&A Monitor DataBase bid premia tables for UK and Irish public targets, 2008 to date. Each record is one bidder's approach to one target.
    Firm / possible offers
    Firm offers are records with a status of Completed, Failed or Pending. Possible offers are approaches announced under the Code (named or unnamed offeror) where that bidder never made a firm offer. Years use the firm offer announcement date, or the start of the offer period for possible offers.
    Speculation premium
    Offer value against the undisturbed share price before pre-bid speculation. Possible offers count only where an indicative price was put forward.
    Offer period
    Days from addition to removal from the Takeover Panel disclosure table, counted once per offer period however many bidders were involved. Live offer periods are excluded.
    Firm offer to completion
    Days from the firm offer announcement to completion, for completed offers. The lapse figure uses failed offers.
    PUSU
    Share of offers with a put-up-or-shut-up deadline, among records where this is captured (from 2011). Extensions are measured against offers with a PUSU. Conversion to a firm offer needs possible offers switched on; live PUSUs are excluded.
    Failed
    Failed ÷ (completed + failed) firm offers. Pending offers are excluded.
    Hostile
    Hostile and initially hostile offers ÷ offers with a recorded attitude.
    Competing bids
    Bid situations (target and speculation date) where more than one bidder emerged. The winner comes from the DataBase's competitive status; "neither" means every bidder failed. Unresolved contests are excluded from the win rates.
    Competitive deals
    The Competitive deals criterion uses the same flag as the Competing column: offers the DataBase marks as part of a contested bid situation. With it set to Competitive, the Competing situations figure is 100% by definition, and the Competing bids panel shows who won those contests.
    Increases
    A firm offer counts as increased when its final value per target share (cash plus shares) is above its initial value. The average increase is the mean uplift on increased offers only.
    Price revised
    A bid counts as revised when any of its indicative proposals or formal offers differs from its first price. Bids with no price, or with prices more than 3x apart (probable data-entry errors), drop out when this is set.
    Offer revisions
    The Offer revisions tab compares each bid's price and premium at its first and last offer: for formal offers, the 1st and last Rule 2.7 offer; for possible offers, the 1st indicative proposal and either the last formal offer (if the bid went firm) or the last indicative proposal. Custom stages compares any ticked stages (1st to 5th and last proposal, 1st to 5th and last formal offer) for bids that reached all of them. An offer counts as raised when its last price is above its first; prices more than 3x apart are left out as probable data errors.
    Other criteria
    Scheme = deal type includes a scheme of arrangement; Offer excludes possible offers, whose structure is not yet known. MBO / MBI follows the DataBase deal type. "Incl. shares" covers any share element or share alternative.
